Questions

Before you book

What do I need to have ready?

A working hose spigot and an outdoor or garage outlet. We bring our own hose, cords, water and all equipment. If you are in an apartment or townhome without a spigot, text us first and we will figure it out.

How long does it take?

Plan on 1.5 to 2 hours for a single package and 4 or more hours for multiple vehicles or a ceramic coating. We confirm timing when we walk through the vehicle with you.

What if it rains?

We text you the day before if the forecast looks bad and get you rescheduled fast. Rain does not affect interior-only details.

Can you remove pet hair, stains or odors?

Light pet hair is included in every package. Heavy pet hair, stains and odors are add-ons. We do our best but cannot promise perfection on every stain.

How do I pay, and why does cash skip sales tax?

Card, check or cash, after you inspect the work. Card and check payments are charged Minnesota sales tax. Cash payments are not.

Do you offer discounts?

10% off for military and first responders, mention it at booking. Referrals earn you 10% off your next service once your friend's detail is complete.

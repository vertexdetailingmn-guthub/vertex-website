Who this is for

Busy families, lease returns, and people who hate car washes

Kids, dogs, Minnesota salt, construction dust, a long commute. If your car gets dirty faster than you can deal with it, a plan costs about the same as the car wash subscription you have now and actually cleans the inside.

Leasing or planning to sell? A car that's been maintained every month returns in a different condition than one detailed once before turn-in. Excess wear fees are real money.

What's not included

  • Full shampoo of every seat and carpet. That's the intro detail, and one per season on Monthly.
  • Heavy pet hair, biohazard, or severe stains. Quoted on the spot, at 20% off.
  • Paint correction. We don't offer it.

Common questions

Do I have to be home?

No. We need access to a spigot and an outlet. Most plan customers leave the car in the driveway and we text when we're done.

Can I skip a month?

Yes. Text us before your visit. We ask for 48 hours notice so we can fill the slot.

How do I pay?

Card on file through our booking system, charged after each visit. Or pay cash, check or Venmo at the door.

What about winter?

We don't wash exteriors under 40°F. Your plan pauses in late November and restarts in April. Interior-only winter visits are available on request.

Is there a contract?

No. Cancel by text, any time, no fee. The only condition is that the $150 intro detail requires at least two plan visits afterward, otherwise we bill the difference to the regular price.

Straight talk

What a coating does, and what it doesn't

It does: make the car dramatically easier to clean, protect against UV fading, salt, bird droppings, tree sap and chemical etching, and add real depth and gloss. A coated car washed every few weeks looks new for years.

It doesn't: fix swirl marks or scratches that are already there. A coating locks in whatever's underneath it. We don't offer paint correction, so if your paint has heavy swirls we'll tell you honestly and may suggest a correction shop first. On most daily drivers under 5 years old, the paint is fine to coat as-is.

It's not: bulletproof. Rocks still chip. Automatic car washes with brushes will still scratch. Treat it like a very good paint, not armor.

After-care, in two sentences

Don't wash it for 7 days. After that, hand wash or touchless only, every 2 to 4 weeks, and we'll reapply the topper on a maintenance plan.

Best time to book

Fall. A coating applied in September or October goes into its first Minnesota winter protected, and salt is the whole reason to do this. Spring is the second-best time. We don't coat under 45°F.

What to expect

Your driveway, our equipment

We bring our own hose, cords, water tank, vacuums, steamer and every chemical. All we need from you is a working spigot and an outdoor or garage outlet. If you're in an apartment or townhome without one, text us first and we'll work it out.

You get a text when we're on the way. We walk the vehicle with you first so the price and the plan are agreed before we start. When we're done, you inspect it. If you spot something, we fix it before we leave. Then you pay by card, check, Venmo, or cash. Cash skips sales tax.

Weather

Rain doesn't stop an interior. Exteriors get rescheduled if it's pouring or under 40°F, and we text you the day before if it looks iffy.

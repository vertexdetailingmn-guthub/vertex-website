Honest limits

What we don't do

  • Oxidation removal and gelcoat compounding. If your hull is chalky and faded, that's a multi-day job with heavy machine polishing. We don't offer it, and a coating over oxidized gelcoat won't hold. We'll tell you if that's what you've got.
  • Boats over 24 feet. Cabin cruisers and anything that needs a lift or a yard. We're mobile and we work off a trailer in your driveway or at your dock.
  • Winterization and mechanical work. We used to offer it. We don't anymore. Your marina or a mobile marine mechanic handles that.
  • Class A motorhomes. Too tall for our setup to do right.

Everything else, from a 16-foot pontoon to a 22-foot wake boat or a 30-foot travel trailer, is a normal day for us.
